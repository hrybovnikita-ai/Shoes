# Nike colorways: catalog sources

These are the 25 Nike products seeded by `backend/data/nikeColorways.js` (ids 101–125, 5 per colour family). For each one the official name, style code (SKU) and colorway were checked against release coverage and retailer listings. Every price is the US retail price (launch MSRP unless the notes say otherwise), not a resale price.

**How it was verified (2026-11-23).**

- **Names, colorways and style codes:** matched against at least two independent sources per item: release blogs (Sneaker Files, Sneaker Bar Detroit, House of Heat, Sneaker News, Nice Kicks) plus a retailer listing with structured product data (KicksCrew JSON-LD `sku`/`colorway`).
- **Prices:** taken from the release coverage. StockX, GOAT and Sneaker News block automated fetches, so links to them are references and were not fetched.
- **Images:** all images are product photos from KicksCrew's Shopify CDN (`cdn.shopify.com`). Each URL returned HTTP 200 with `image/jpeg`, and each photo was checked by eye against its model and colour.
- **Licensing caveat:** the images are hot-linked from a third-party retailer. Before going live, replace them with Nike or authorised press images, or self-host images you have rights to.

**Store data.** Stock levels are demo inventory, not real availability. Ratings and `old_price` are left NULL, so there are no invented reviews or discounts. Men's items list EU 39–46 and women's items (101, 116) list EU 36–42. Checkout enforces these sizes.

## Substitutions and labels to know about

- **Purple Air Max 270 → women's 'Lilac' (DZ5206-500).** The men's purple AM270s are mostly black or are 'Throwback Future' themed.
- **Yellow Air Max 270 → women's 'Yellow Strike' (DQ4694-700).**
- **Green Air Max Plus → 'Volt' (CV8838-300).** Nike calls it Volt, not green. It's shown under Green because Volt is a yellow-green.
- **Black Dunk Low → 'Panda' (DD1391-100), which is black/white.** No general-release all-black Dunk Low exists, so a 'triple black Dunk' was not invented.
- **Black Air Jordan 1 → 2014 'Black/White' (555088-010).**
- **Black filter count:** the two original black products (ids 3 and 5) are also black, so the Black filter shows 7 items: 5 new plus 2 existing.

No item was left unverified: all 25 have a confirmed official name and style code. Where sources disagree on price, the notes give both figures.
